# Borrowing
source: https://lib.hku.hk/FAQ/Borrowing/index.html

Q: How many books can I borrow?
A: Loan limits vary by patron status and material type. Academic staff may borrow up to 400 books for 180 days, postgraduates 180 books for 180 days, and undergraduates 60 books for 60 days. Full details at http://lib.hku.hk/general/borrowing

Q: Where can I checkout books?
A: Books can either be borrowed at the circulation counters or through the self-check stations. Bring your Student, Staff or Facilities Access Card.

Q: Where can I return books to the library?
A: Items with loan periods of 15 days or more can be returned to any HKU Library location. Short-loan items of 14 days or less must be returned to their original service point. Book drops and automated book returns are available at the entrance of selected libraries. Audio-visual items, equipment and special items must be returned to their original location.

Q: Can I renew books online?
A: Yes. Renewals can be made from MyAccount@HKUL in Find@HKUL, using your HKU Portal UID or library card number and PIN. Renewals are not available if another patron has placed a hold, or if the maximum renewal period has been reached.

Q: Can I request books borrowed by others?
A: Yes. Log into Find@HKUL to place a hold on a borrowed item. This triggers a recall and notifies the current borrower, whose loan period may be shortened accordingly.

Q: Can I pick up a Medical Library book in the Main Library?
A: Yes. An interbranch delivery service operates between on-campus and off-campus libraries. Request eligible items through Find@HKUL for delivery to your preferred location.

Q: What if my books are overdue?
A: Overdues are subject to fines on a scale laid down by the Senate Library Committee. The Libraries send reminder emails, but patrons must monitor their accounts regularly. See http://lib.hku.hk/cdd/br-overdue.html

Q: Can I request books from other libraries?
A: Eligible users can access Interlibrary Loan (ILL) and Article Delivery Services through Find@HKUL for materials held by local and overseas institutions.
