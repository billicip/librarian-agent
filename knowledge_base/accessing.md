# Accessing the Libraries
source: https://lib.hku.hk/FAQ/index.html

Q: How can I access the HKU Libraries?
A: A valid HKU student or staff card grants access. HKU Alumni and eligible HKU SPACE students can apply for a library card. Members of the public may obtain Circle of Friends membership through the user information portal.

Q: What authentication is required for web access?
A: HKU staff, SPACE staff, and students should login with their HKU Portal UID and PIN. Other patrons use their library card number and PIN instead.

Q: What are the Libraries opening hours?
A: Library hours vary during the year, with longer hours during term time. Current schedules are posted in the Hours section of the Libraries Homepage at https://lib.hku.hk
