# Equipment and Study Places
source: https://lib.hku.hk/FAQ/Study/index.html

Q: Are there computers installed with Microsoft Office?
A: Yes. The Technology Zone on Level 3 offers over 100 computers providing access to FIND@HKUL, databases, the Internet, and application software including Microsoft Office, SPSS and NVivo on selected machines. Laptops and tablets are available for short-term loan at the Main Library and branches, but cannot be checked out overnight.

Q: Are there printers in the library?
A: The uPrint service is available in the Main Library and all Branch Libraries. Use your HKU staff or student access card; retrieve print jobs at any printer by touching your card on the smart card pad. Printing units can be added through the HKU Portal, the app, Octopus cards at ITS Add Value Machines, or online for facilities cardholders.

Q: Are there photocopiers in the library?
A: uPrint-operated copiers are available in the Main Library and all branch libraries. Photocopying costs $0.3 for black and white and $1.5 for colour. Patrons must comply with copyright guidelines.

Q: Where can I study in the library?
A: Study tables are on each floor in the Main Library and branch libraries. Level 3's Study Zone offers a variety of single study places for private and reflective research. The Tin Ka Ping Education Library has 18 discussion rooms supporting group learning. Online booking of these facilities is available under Libraries Homepage > Book A Space.
