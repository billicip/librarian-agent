# Research
source: https://lib.hku.hk/FAQ/Research/index.html

Q: How do I find research information for my assignments?
A: Enter your keywords into the search box on the Libraries' Homepage. Through the discovery tool FIND@HKUL you can find books and articles in one go. For deeper exploration, consult specific databases under Electronic Resources.

Q: How do I find past exam papers?
A: ExamBase provides full text of HKU past examination papers. Access it via HKUL Homepage > Tools > HKUL Digital Initiatives. You can search by paper title, course number or full text.

Q: How can I effectively search electronic resources?
A: Join the library courses available throughout term time; the schedule is online. The Libraries also offer Subject Guides with online instructions on using databases and other resources. See https://libguides.lib.hku.hk

Q: What if I need assistance in a specific subject area?
A: Contact your Subject Librarian. They develop collections and services for assigned subject areas and act as the Libraries' liaison to their faculties. A Research Consultation Service offering one-on-one advisory support is also available for eligible patrons.

Q: Can I access the electronic resources from home?
A: HKU staff, students and HKU SPACE staff can access e-resources remotely by authenticating with their HKU Portal UID and PIN.

Q: With my wireless mobile device, can I surf the Web in the Library?
A: The HKU Libraries has wireless hotspots covering all seating areas. HKU staff and students should configure their devices for the "HKU" or "eduroam" connection.

Q: How can I find my course materials?
A: Some professors prepare online reading lists, accessible from ReadingList@HKUL or Moodle.

Q: How can I get organised with my references?
A: Use EndNote, the bibliographic software that helps you manage references and cite while you write. Current HKU users can download it and attend workshops to learn its functionality.
